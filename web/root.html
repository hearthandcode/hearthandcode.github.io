OPEN THE REVIEW SURFACE ↗

EVIDENCE CLASSES

Every page labels its claims.

The same eight epistemic labels appear throughout the site. They signal what kind of claim is being made and prevent a passing check from being mistaken for a final verdict.

SOURCE

A named, versioned artifact that the claim depends on — a specification, a charter, a committed document.

EVIDENCE

An observed result from a direct check or measurement — build output, test result, rendered page.

INFERENCE

A reasoned conclusion from evidence with visible assumptions — patterns drawn from observed practice.

HYPOTHESIS

A testable explanation with alternatives and a possible falsifier — ideas that are explicitly provisional.

PROPOSAL

A candidate artifact or design awaiting disposition — drafts, designs, recommendations.

PROJECTION

A derived view that does not replace its source — dashboards, summaries, rendered pages.

RECEIPT

Bounded process history — what happened, not what it authorizes for the future.

UNKNOWN

Cannot be reliably classified — a gap shown as a gap, not hidden.

THE OPERATING POSTURE

A useful answer carries its own boundaries.

  1. 01Bind

    Use a direct source, a declared task and a clear owner. Know what the work is answering and what it cannot change.

  2. 02Make

    Produce a small, inspectable artifact with a working seam. Let its promises be small enough to test.

  3. 03Check

    Test the stated predicate independently of fluent explanation. Include the counterexample that keeps the claim honest.

  4. 04Return

    Keep evidence, inference, proposal and release distinct. A useful return names the next question as clearly as it names what was done.

CLAIM BOUNDARY

What this site does not assert.

  • No page in this orientation asserts verification, product completion, operational deployment, provider availability or third-party adoption.
  • Candidate and proposal labels mark material that has not been accepted, released or verified.
  • Synthesized examples demonstrate a method; they do not trace to a releasable source record and should not be cited as empirical evidence.
  • Site content describes working approaches and candidate designs. It does not constitute a binding standard, a governance implementation or a warranty of fitness for any purpose.
  • Readers evaluating any technique for their own work should independently verify that it meets their requirements, constraints and safety needs.
